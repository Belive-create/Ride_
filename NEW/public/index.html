What Sarathi does, who does the work, and where the money is.

An agent you text on Telegram like a person. It answers what it can itself for free (₹0) and hires a human for physical store calls, app testing, or legal advice — with money handled end-to-end via UPI Escrow.
One of three. AI handles digital drafting and answers free. Hands-on micro-tasks (store phone calls, app testing, regional translation) go to the Sarathi worker network. Credentialed legal or tax tasks route to vetted Indian CAs and Advocates.
When a human job is quoted, you confirm via Telegram. A pre-authorization holds funds in escrow. Money is never released until the worker submits proof on the web portal and AI verifies the deliverable.
Gig workers and specialists log into /worker.html to view escrow-funded jobs, claim tasks, submit completion proof notes, and track their instant UPI payouts.
When a worker submits proof, Google Gemini Flash automatically evaluates the submitted proof against the original task prompt to verify accuracy before triggering the UPI payout.
